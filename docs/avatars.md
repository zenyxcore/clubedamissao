## Prompt do personagem 01

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE finished square character avatar for Clube da Missão, a premium family treasure-hunt adventure app. Original storybook animation character art: polished hand-painted 3D-inspired digital illustration, expressive warm eyes, soft sculpted shapes, detailed curly hair, beautiful cinematic golden rim light, warm skin tones, rich forest green and dark teal with brass gold accents. Head and upper shoulders portrait, looking toward viewer with friendly adventurous smile; face large and readable at 64 pixels, entire head inside generous safe padding, shoulders extend to lower edge, perfectly centered and suitable for circular cropping. Deep petroleum teal #092A35 soft atmospheric background with very subtle forest bokeh, no objects floating behind. Outfit: forest-green explorer vest, mustard neckerchief, tiny brass compass pin, tan shirt, no text. Subject: a joyful 7-year-old Black girl with deep brown skin, two natural braided hair puffs, small brass explorer goggles resting above her forehead, kind curious expression. No writing, no numbers, no logo, no border, no collage, no extra characters. Single production-ready avatar image.
